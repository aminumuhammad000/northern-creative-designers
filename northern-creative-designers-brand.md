# Northern Creative Designers brand guidelines

This file describes how Northern Creative Designers looks and sounds. When you design, build or write anything for Northern Creative Designers, follow it: use only the colors and typefaces listed here, and write in the voice it describes. If something is not covered, say so and ask, do not invent a rule.

## Introduction

### Overview

The Northern Creatives Designers Community is an initiative focused on the advancement and connectivity of design professionals within Northern Nigeria. We provide a structured environment for networking, knowledge acquisition, and career advancement. Our activities encompass the curation of development resources, the organization of skill-enhancing workshops, the provision of constructive critique mechanisms, and the dissemination of professional opportunities. By fostering collaboration and facilitating access to vital resources, we are committed to strengthening the design landscape in the Northern region. We encourage dedicated design practitioners to become a part of our growing network.

### Who we are

The Northern Creatives Designers Community is an initiative focused on the advancement and connectivity of design professionals within Northern Nigeria. We provide a structured environment for networking, knowledge acquisition, and career advancement.

### Brand Purpose

Three beliefs sit behind everything we make
- **Skill Enhancement & Learning:** ffering diverse opportunities for skill development through workshops, masterclasses, guest speakers, peer-to-peer learning, curated resources, and constructive critique sessions.
- **Networking & Collaboration:** Facilitating connections and teamwork through online platforms, virtual meetups, collaborative projects, a mentorship program, and industry connections.
- **Business & Career Development:** Providing resources and guidance for professional growth via portfolio reviews, freelancing support, a job board, business skills workshops, and promotional opportunities.
- **Community Building & Support:** Fostering a strong and inclusive environment through a welcoming onboarding process, regular communication, recognition of achievements, a resource library, and inspirational content.

### Target Audience

The Northern Creative Designers community serves a diverse spectrum of individuals and entities, unified by a commitment to the growth of the design industry in Northern Nigeria. The Northern Creative Designers community likely consists of: • Graphic designers • Brand designers • UI/UX designers • Product designers • Motion Designers • Fashion Designers • Digital artists From Northern Nigeria, or those interested in the region's creative industry.

## Strategy

### Mission

To be the premier catalyst for a thriving, globally competitive design industry in Northern Nigeria, where local talent is recognized as a cornerstone of regional innovation and economic development.

### Vision

To empower Northern Nigerian design professionals by providing a structured ecosystem for skill acquisition, collaborative networking, and career growth. We are committed to bridging the resource gap and fostering an inclusive community where creativity meets professional excellence

## Color

### Proportion

How much of each color to use: Spring 50%, Pine 27%, Tangerine 13%, White 7%, Navy 3%.

### Contrast

The strongest text and background pairs:
- Navy (#140949) on White (#FFFFFF): 18.0:1, AAA
- White (#FFFFFF) on Navy (#140949): 18.0:1, AAA
- White (#FFFFFF) on Pine (#013811): 13.3:1, AAA
- Pine (#013811) on White (#FFFFFF): 13.3:1, AAA
- Navy (#140949) on Spring (#03D06B): 8.8:1, AAA
- Spring (#03D06B) on Navy (#140949): 8.8:1, AAA
- Navy (#140949) on Tangerine (#FF8500): 7.4:1, AAA
- Pine (#013811) on Spring (#03D06B): 6.5:1, AA
Any other pair must have a contrast of at least 4.5:1 for text, or 3:1 for large headings.

## Typography

### Hierarchy

| Role | Size | Line height | Tracking | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | 64px | 1 | -2.5% |  |
| Heading | 40px | 1.1 | -1.5% |  |
| Subheading | 24px | 1.25 | 0% |  |
| Body | 16px | 1.4 | 0% |  |
| Caption | 12px | 1.4 | 1% |  |
Line height and tracking are what each style is set in. A style follows its typeface's line height and letter spacing for headlines or text, unless the value is marked (own): set for that style alone, it stays when the typeface's changes.
